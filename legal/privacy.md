## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address, company (optional) and a password. We store only a secure hash of your password, never the password itself.

- **Messages:** what you write in the contact and pricing forms, with your name, email and company.
- **Orders:** the details you give when you ask to buy a report.

## Why we use it, and our legal basis

- To run your account and give you access to the plan you have (contract).
- To reply to your messages and arrange orders and subscriptions (contract and legitimate interests).
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interests).
- To send you research updates and news by email, only if you ticked the mailing list box (consent). You can withdraw consent at any time by unticking the box in your account or by contacting us. Consent is separate from agreeing to the terms and is never required to use the site.

## Cookies

We use one cookie, a session cookie that keeps you signed in and protects forms from forgery. It is strictly necessary, so it does not need consent, and it is removed when you close your browser. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.
