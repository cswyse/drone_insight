Move forward

Advancing changes the headline year when the year rolls over and extends the forecast by a year. New years are filled in from the growth of your existing market sizes, so there is something to show straight away: enter real figures in Market sizing when you have them. Your “reported to” dates do not move, so the new period shows as an estimate until your data arrives.

When new data arrives

Marking a year or quarter as reported changes its label from estimate to actual, and new financials you enter for it default to “Reported”. It does not change any numbers.
